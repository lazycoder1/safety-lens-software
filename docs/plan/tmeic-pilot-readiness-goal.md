Updated: 2026-06-24

## Scope

This goal converts the TMEIC UPS manufacturing requirements into an evidence-backed pilot plan. It uses the customer-specific requirements analysis, the Rakshak Lens PRD/base-pack requirements, the current sales-readiness report, the scenario manifest, and existing TMEIC videos under `test-videos/tmeic/`.

Current delivery decision: finish the 9 `can_do_now` requirements for the immediate TMEIC pilot. The other 12 requirements remain documented in the matrix, but are not part of the tomorrow deliverable package because they need footage, model tuning, compound runtime logic, physical integration proof, privacy/legal approval, or Jetson/site evidence.

Hospitals/RL-M remain out of scope. Fall/person-down is re-scoped here as a factory safety pilot only, not clinical monitoring. For TMEIC, “fall of person” includes an already-fallen/person-down posture alert; it does not require footage of the falling motion itself. Animal detection is dog/cat ready; snake is tracked separately because it is not proven by the current COCO animal scenarios.

## Evidence Rules

- Every claimed detection needs one positive run, one false-positive guard, and one inactive detector-window run.
- YAML/config is the only allowed setup path for camera/rule/runtime changes.
- Alert-only schedules are not enough for detector-off claims; evidence must show suppressed capability telemetry and zero model invocations/candidates.
- Jetson/site evidence is required before production or 10-camera claims.

## TMEIC-05 Model/Data Research Note

Checked current public options on 2026-06-25. YOLOE remains the right local exploratory path because current Ultralytics docs list YOLOE-26 text-prompt models and describe text-prompted open-vocabulary detection. Public syringe and pill datasets exist, including Roboflow syringe examples and the Ultralytics medical-pills dataset, but these are not enough to sell TMEIC factory CCTV detection: pills/syringes are small objects, likely hand-occluded, and visually easy to confuse with tools, packets, reflections, and shopfloor clutter. Treat the new TMEIC PE Stores negative-control and detector-window runs as runtime plumbing evidence only. Before pilot: get TMEIC/legal approval for the use case, define acceptable-use boundaries, collect positive site clips or approved staged footage, add hard negatives, and validate a tuned/exportable model on Mac M1 first and Jetson Nano target hardware before customer claims.
